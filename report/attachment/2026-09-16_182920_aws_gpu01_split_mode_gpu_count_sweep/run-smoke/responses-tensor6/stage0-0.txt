The capital of France is **Paris**, and the capital of Germany is **Berlin**.

Paris has served as the political and cultural center of France for centuries, while Berlin became the capital of a unified Germany in 1990 following the fall of the Berlin Wall. Prior to reunification, West Germany's capital was Bonn, and East Germany's capital was East Berlin. Today, Berlin is the seat of the federal government and the Bundestag (parliament).
