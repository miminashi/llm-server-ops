### Travel Tips (Brief)

#### Paris
- **Best Time to Visit**: Spring (April–June) and Fall (September–November) for mild weather.
- **Must-See**: Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Montmartre, Seine River cruise.
- **Food**: Croissants, baguettes, macarons, French onion soup.

#### Berlin
- **Best Time to Visit**: Spring (May–June) and Summer (July–August) for outdoor festivals.
- **Must-See**: Brandenburg Gate, Museum Island, East Side Gallery, Tiergarten Park.
- **Food**: Currywurst, Bratwurst, Berliner Pfannkuchen (pancakes).
